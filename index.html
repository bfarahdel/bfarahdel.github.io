Background & Engineering

With an MS in Computer Science (Georgia State) and a BS in Biomedical Engineering (Georgia Tech), I love using software to solve real-world problems—whether that’s analyzing health data or building clean, user-focused web apps.

Research & Data Science

My research focuses on neuroimaging at the TReNDS Center (published in IEEE EMBC) and genomics quality control tools for Memorial Sloan Kettering. I enjoy translating complex medical datasets into actionable insights and tools.
